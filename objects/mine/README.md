# Your own things

Put your own pictures here: a photo of your grandmother, your dog, a ticket stub, a drawing.
PNG, JPG or WEBP, under 5 MB each. A picture with a see-through background sits on the shelf best.

Then add it to your altar by its file name:

```json
{ "image": "nana.png", "label": "nana" }
```

Your pictures stay on your computer. The build tucks them inside your altar.html so the page needs nothing else.
