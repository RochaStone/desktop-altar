Arrange your altar.

  1. 01Drag things from the drawer onto a shelf. Or click a thing to place it on the first shelf with room.
  2. 02Give a thing a little label if you like. Use the arrows to move it, and the cross to put it back.
  3. 03Write your intention and your daily lines on the right, then press save.
